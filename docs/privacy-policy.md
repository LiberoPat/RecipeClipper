# Recipe Clipper privacy policy

*Draft, not yet published. Last updated: [date of publication].*

Recipe Clipper takes a recipe link you share with it and shows you just the
recipe. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

## What we collect

Nothing. Recipe Clipper has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no
tracking and no servers of its own. We, the developer, never receive your
recipes, your settings, the links you share or anything else about you or
your device.

## What stays on your device

- **Recipes you open**: the link, the recipe's text and the time you last
  viewed it, kept so they appear in History and in your lists. History keeps
  the 50 most recently viewed recipes that aren't in a list; the rest are
  deleted automatically.
- **Your lists**, and which recipes are in them.
- **Your settings**: units, oven temperature and the cook-mode theme.
- **Recipe photos**, cached so they show when you're offline.

You can delete any recipe from History or from the recipe screen. Deleting
the app deletes everything it stored.

If your phone's own backup is turned on (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google
backup on Android), the operating system may include this data in your
backup, stored in your own account under Apple's or Google's terms. We have
no access to it.

## Network connections

When you share or open a link, Recipe Clipper downloads that page directly
from the recipe's website, and its photo from wherever the page says it is
hosted, the same as a web browser would. Those sites see an ordinary request
from your device, including your IP address, and their own privacy policies
apply. Recipe Clipper sends them nothing else: no cookies from other sites,
no identifiers, and no information about you.

The app makes no other connections.

## Sharing a recipe out

When you share a recipe from the app, it goes as plain text through your
phone's share sheet to the app you choose. Recipe Clipper doesn't see where
it goes.

## Crash reports

The app contains no crash-reporting or analytics code. If you have chosen in
your phone's settings to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or
Google may pass us anonymous crash reports under their terms. You can turn
that off in your phone's settings.

## Children

Recipe Clipper collects no personal information from anyone, including
children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
