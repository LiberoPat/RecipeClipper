## Description (both stores)

Up to 4,000 characters on each store.

> **Skip the story. Cook.**
>
> Share a recipe link from your browser or any app, and Pith shows just the recipe:
> the ingredients, the steps, the times and the servings. No life story, no ads, no pop-ups.
>
> **Free. No ads. No account. No subscription.**
>
> - **Every recipe stays.** Your recipes are kept on your phone and open offline, photos
>   included. Search them by title or ingredient, and sort them into your own lists.
> - **Scale and convert.** Change the servings and every amount follows. Show amounts as
>   written, in metric or in ounces, and oven temperatures in °C or °F. Anything that can't be
>   converted exactly stays as written.
> - **Cook mode.** One step at a time, with timers that still ring when you leave the app, and
>   the screen kept on.
> - **Plan, shop, and use what you have.** Plan the week's meals, turn them into a grocery
>   list, and keep a pantry so you buy only what you need.
> - **Bring recipes in any way.** A link, a photo of a recipe card or a cookbook page, text you
>   copied, or one you type in. Ask your own AI chat app for ideas and bring its answer back.
> - **Yours, on your device.** No account and no server: nothing you save leaves your phone
>   unless you share it. Back it up, export it, or send a recipe to a friend.
