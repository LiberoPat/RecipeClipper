Creamy Tuscan Chicken

By Jo · May 2, 2024

Sun-dried tomatoes, spinach and a splash of cream make this a weeknight favourite.

Creamy Tuscan Chicken

Yields: 4 servings

Total Time: 35 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 (6- to 8-oz.) boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • Kosher salt
  • 3 Tbsp. unsalted butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 1/2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 cups baby spinach
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan

Directions

  1. In a large skillet over medium heat, heat oil. Season the chicken with salt and cook until golden, 8 minutes per side.
  2. Melt the butter in the same skillet and add the garlic. Stir in the tomatoes and cook until they burst.
  3. Add the spinach and cook until wilted. Stir in the cream and Parmesan and bring to a simmer.
  4. Return the chicken to the skillet and cook until heated through, 5 to 7 minutes.

Creamy Tuscan Orzo

Total Time: 30 mins

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp. unsalted butter
  • 1 cup orzo
  • 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 cups baby spinach

Directions

  1. Melt the butter in a large pot and toast the orzo until golden, 3 minutes.
  2. Add the broth, bring to a boil and cook until the orzo is tender, 10 minutes.
  3. Stir in the cream and spinach until wilted.
