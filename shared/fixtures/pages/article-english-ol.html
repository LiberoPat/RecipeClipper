The lemon bars my neighbour taught me

Every summer she made a tray of these, and every summer the street ate them before they cooled.

You will need:

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened
  • 2 cups flour
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • Zest of 2 lemons

Method:

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Press the butter, flour and sugar into a 9x13 pan and bake for 20 minutes.
  3. Whisk the eggs and zest, pour over the crust and bake 25 minutes more.

Notes:

They keep for 3 days in the fridge.
