How to keep herbs fresh

Soft herbs wilt within a day unless they are stored well. Three things help:

  • Trim the stems as soon as you get home.
  • Stand them in a glass of water like flowers.
  • Cover them loosely with a bag in the fridge.
  • Use 2 or 3 sprigs at a time.

Change the water every day and they will last a week.

Basil is the exception: keep it out of the fridge.
