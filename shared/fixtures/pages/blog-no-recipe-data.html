Grandma’s Banana Bread

By Jo · March 3, 2024

There is something about the smell of banana bread that takes me straight back to my grandmother’s kitchen. She baked it every Sunday, and I have made it 12 times this month alone.

Ingredients you’ll need

Very ripe bananas are the secret. The browner the better: they bring sweetness and moisture. You’ll also need butter, sugar, an egg and flour, which you probably have already.

Tips for the best loaf

Don’t overmix the batter. A few streaks of flour are fine, and the loaf stays tender.

Grandma’s Banana Bread

Prep Time: 15 minutes Cook Time: 1 hour

Servings: 10 slices

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
  • ⅓ cup melted butter
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and butter a 4x8-inch loaf pan.
  2. In a mixing bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork until smooth. Stir in the melted butter.
  3. Mix in the baking soda and salt. Stir in the sugar, beaten egg and vanilla extract.
  4. Mix in the flour.
  5. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan. Bake for 55 to 65 minutes, until a tester comes out clean.
  6. Remove from the oven and let cool in the pan for a few minutes. Then remove and cool on a rack.

Notes: freezes well for up to 3 months.
