Two dips for a party

Both keep for two days in the fridge, so make them the day before.

Smoky hummus

  • 1 can (400 g) chickpeas, drained
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika

Blend everything with a splash of water until smooth.

Taste, add salt, and finish with more paprika.

Herb yogurt dip

A cool one to go beside it:

  • 250 g Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  1. Stir the dill and garlic into the yogurt.
  2. Chill for 30 minutes.

What to serve them with

  • Pita chips
  • Carrot sticks
  • Radishes
